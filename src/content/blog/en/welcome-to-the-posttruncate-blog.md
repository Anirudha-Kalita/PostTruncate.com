## Why character limits matter

Every platform cuts your copy at a different point. A post that reads perfectly in
your editor can get clipped mid-sentence in the feed, hiding your call to action
right where it counts. Knowing each limit **before** you publish keeps your hook intact.

## What you'll find here

- Plain-English breakdowns of each platform's limits
- Formatting tips that survive the "...see more" fold
- Notes on how links, emoji, and line breaks count toward your total

> The goal is simple: write once, and know exactly how it will look everywhere.
